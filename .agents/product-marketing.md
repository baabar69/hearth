## Product Overview

- **One line:** Hearth is a membership that pairs you with one trained listener (a Keeper), matched by hand, who stays for years.
- **What it does:** A 35 to 60 minute video or audio call every two weeks (the Sit), a chat thread in between (the Long Talk, replies within a day), and a short written note from your Keeper every Friday. Small-group sessions (Circles) and a referral path to vetted therapists (The Bridge) sit around that core.
- **Category shelf:** peer support, the private alternative to therapy apps. Not therapy, not coaching, not a chatbot.
- **Type:** consumer subscription service. Web app at dearhearth.com, checkout via Whop.
- **Pricing:** Hearthside $39/month (call every two weeks) or $390/year. Hearth Deep $99/month (call every week) or $990/year. Cancel any time, one click.

## Target Audience

- **Primary (Instagram, first 90 days):** South Asian diaspora adults in the US and Canada, 28 to 45. Archetype: the eldest daughter who has been the family's emotional infrastructure for fifteen years. Educated, employed, often in caregiving-heavy work, often a partner or parent. Not in crisis. Quietly tired in a way she cannot explain to anyone in her life without hurting them.
- **Secondary:** the first-generation man who was never taught to grieve (35 to 55); the new parent losing her identity between two cultures (28 to 40); the sandwich-generation caregiver parenting kids and parents across continents (40 to 60).
- **Widen later:** any US or Canadian adult carrying everyday weight (grief, family, loneliness, the in-between years). Positioning in `docs/business.md` is universal; Instagram goes narrow first to get traction.
- **Jobs to be done:** (1) have one person who knows my whole story so I stop explaining; (2) have somewhere to put the week so it stops leaking into everything else; (3) feel heard by someone who notices what I did not say.

## Problems and Pain Points

- Known only in pieces: one version at work, one at home, one for the friend who gets the edited story.
- The person everyone leans on; nobody asks how they are.
- Therapy felt clinical, expensive, or wrong for weight that is not a diagnosis. Friends get tired and are part of the weight.
- Cost of doing nothing: the brave face, exhaustion, the same family fight for ten years.
- Emotional tension: guilt about needing support, fear of being dramatic, not wanting to burden anyone, shame about saying it out loud.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Direct:** BetterHelp, Talkspace (online therapy; licensed clinicians; rotating providers; roughly $280 to $400/month for BetterHelp per their FAQ, Aug 2026). 7 Cups (free volunteer listeners, anonymous, rotating).
- **Secondary:** Calm and Headspace (one-way, no listener), AI companions (Replika, Pi: always on, not a person).
- **Indirect:** calling a friend, a 12-week group program, doing nothing.
- **Where they fall short:** no continuity (you can be reassigned), matched by algorithm or availability, priced for crisis not for years, nobody carries your story forward.

## Differentiation

1. **One person, kept for years.** Same Keeper in month one and year three.
2. **Matched by a person, within 72 hours.** A human reads the intake and chooses for fit, language and lived context. If the first Sit is not the right fit, ask and we assign someone else, at no charge.
3. **A shape, not just sessions.** Call every two weeks, chat between, a Friday note.
   Product rule (founder, 2026-08-22): members do not choose their Keeper. Hearth matches one Keeper by hand within 72 hours; if the first Sit is not the right fit, the member asks and Hearth assigns someone else, no questions, no extra charge; later changes remain on request (founder refinement, 2026-08-22; never write "30 days"). Never write "choose your Keeper", "browse Keepers", "pick" or anything implying a directory. Safe phrasing: "matched with one Keeper, kept for as long as it serves you, changeable on request after the first Sit".
4. **Priced to hold for years.** $39/month.
5. **Honest about scope.** Not therapy, and a warm handoff to a vetted therapist when therapy is what is needed.

## Objections and Anti-Personas

- "Is this therapy?" No. Peer support. If you need treatment, we say so and help you find it.
- "Are Keepers qualified?" 120 hours of training, background checks, crisis protocol, monthly supervision, fewer than 8% of applicants accepted. Not clinicians, and clear about it.
- "Why not just call a friend?" Friends get tired and are part of the weight. A Keeper is trained, paid, and not in your life.
- "Is it safe to say this to a company in Pakistan?" Encrypted, not a medical record, export or delete any time. The company is based in Pakistan and says so; Keepers are matched for your context and time zone.
- **Not for:** anyone in active crisis (route to 988 / Samaritans and The Bridge), anyone seeking diagnosis or medication, minors.

## Switching Dynamics (JTBD four forces)

- **Push:** therapy cost, rotating providers, the friend who is tired, the family fight with no witness.
- **Pull:** someone who stays, relief from explaining, a Friday note that says "I noticed".
- **Habit:** managing alone has worked, sort of; journaling; podcasts.
- **Anxiety:** "Am I being dramatic?", "Is this a real company?", "Will it feel like a stranger?"

## Customer Language

- **Problem, verbatim:** "I'm the one everyone calls." "I've told this story so many times." "I don't need a therapist, I need someone to talk to." "My mother brought it up again." "I laugh when nothing is funny."
- **Solution, verbatim:** "She just stayed." "Not having to explain." "He'd done a version of this himself." "It feels like keeping her with me."
- **Use:** relief, comfort, heard, steadier, lighter, less alone, someone who stays, the whole story, carry it with you.
- **Avoid:** healing journey, self-care, trauma (as a hook), happiness, cure, treat, fix, "mental health" as a label for the product, streaks, "safe space".
- **Glossary:** Keeper (your trained listener), the Sit (your call), the Long Talk (the chat thread), the Friday note, Circle (small-group session), The Bridge (help finding a therapist).

## Brand Voice

- Plain sentence first, warmth second. Professional, quiet, specific. Never chirpy.
- No em dashes. No exclamation marks. No "Hi guys". No therapy-influencer cadence.
- One true thing per piece. Specific images (the Sunday call, the kitchen at 11pm) over abstractions.
- Three adjectives: steady, literate, honest.

## Goals

- **Primary:** paid members in the US and Canada.
- **Key conversion:** start the 12-minute intake at dearhearth.com/intake. The funnel since 2026-08-22 (KAN-72): intake, then a membership (checkout carries the email through), then a person matches by hand within 72 hours of both, then the Keeper writes to the member. A welcome email goes out on payment and the founder is notified within a second of any payment, cancellation or refund, so "link in bio" and "DM us" flows can point at /intake with confidence. Nobody is matched before payment and a full intake; never write copy that implies otherwise.
- **Instagram metrics that matter:** sends per reach, saves, non-follower share of reach, US + Canada share of reach (Insights > Audience and per-post Reach by country), profile visits to link clicks, intake starts from the `ig` UTM.
- **Current metrics:** none yet. First milestone is any US/CA share of reach above 50% on trial reels.

## Operating rules adopted from the Saraev course (2026-08-22)

- Do the process by hand once and like the output before automating it. Automate the bottleneck only.
- Prompt, then skill, then loop, then cloud routine. Turn anything said twice into a skill.
- Generate many, select few: plan on four generations per usable output; a human picks.
- Fuzzy variables: human-written templates with 5 to 10 AI-written words per slot, descriptive camelCase names, never whole AI-written messages.
- Credentials up front as environment variables; every skill carries the self-healing clause; failures go to docs/marketing/errors.md.
- Never automate the step where a member must feel valued. Drafts are automated; sending stays human. No SMS to members.
