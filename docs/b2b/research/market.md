## 0. What the market says, in five lines

1. **Benefits money is the hardest money to win in 2026 and 2027.**
   - Costs are rising at the fastest rate in about 20 years.
   - Cost is now the top benefits criterion for most employers.
   - Employers are consolidating vendors.
   - The 2027 plan year is already decided.
2. **Small, fast, discretionary money sits outside benefits procurement.** ERG, heritage-month, awareness-day and learning budgets pay $1,000 to $2,500 for a virtual session. One or two people decide, within weeks. A culturally specific provider (Anise Health) has turned that route into a year-long contract with a Fortune 500 employee group.
3. **"Affordable" is not a winning B2B argument in this category.**
   - EAPs already cost $1 to $3 per employee per month (PEPM), and many come free with disability or life insurance.
   - What employers lack is use (EAP use sits at 3 to 5 percent), cultural competence (offered by only 26 percent of large employers) and measurable outcomes.
4. **An offshore, psychologist-staffed service has to be sold as peer support or coaching, never as psychology or therapy.**
   - US licensure follows the client's location.
   - The title "psychologist" is protected in all 50 states.
   - Ontario applies its psychotherapy rules to providers outside the province.
5. **Cold outreach to companies is legal in both countries, with rules.** Expect about 3 in 100 cold emails and 2 to 3 in 100 cold calls to become a conversation or meeting.

## 2. Sales cycle, procurement and the calendar

### How long deals take

- **B2B sales-cycle roundups for 2026** (vendor analyses: [getboomerang.ai](https://getboomerang.ai/glossaries/b2b-sales-cycle-benchmarks-2026), [optif.ai](https://optif.ai/learn/questions/sales-cycle-length-benchmark/), search summary):
  - Deals under $5,000 close in a median of about 40 days.
  - Mid-market deals take 60 to 120 days.
  - Enterprise deals ($100,000 to $500,000) take 6 to 9 months.
  - Deals above $500,000 take 9 to 12 months or more.
- **Large employers decide benefits once a year.** If you miss the window, "you're looking at another 12 months" ([Rocket Digital Health](https://www.rocketdigitalhealth.com/insights/how-digital-health-companies-can-sell-to-employers), Jan 2023).
- **A real public example:** the School District of Palm Beach County's EAP solicitation 26C-001F ([BoardDocs tabulation](https://go.boarddocs.com/fl/palmbeach/Board.nsf/files/DNCNY9622C1B/$file/ITN%2026C-001F%20Tabulation.pdf), posted 2025-08-15):
  - Advertised 2025-06-30, opened 2025-07-29, presented to the board 2025-10-15.
  - Five-year contract, 2025-10-16 to 2030-10-15, capped at $550,000 a year.
  - Nine proposers: Optum (won), Lyra, Health Advocate, TELUS Health, Spring Health, Lucet, Magellan, Uwill and one local counselling practice.

### What procurement asks for

- **Enterprise readiness.** Buyers expect HITRUST certification and HIPAA compliance, SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, single sign-on, EAP integration, clean eligibility files, outcomes dashboards and customer references ([Calm Health](https://health.calm.com/resources/blog/what-makes-a-mental-health-vendor-enterprise-ready/), 2024 to 2025 data).
- **Security attestations.** "Over 60 percent of enterprise buyers now require vendor security attestations before signing" ([Konfirmity](https://www.konfirmity.com/blog/soc-2-customer-security-questionnaire), vendor claim, 2026). Typical due diligence covers a SOC 2 Type II report, a security questionnaire, HIPAA evidence, cyber liability insurance and incident response.
- **Paperwork for a Pakistan-registered supplier.** Expect supplier-portal onboarding and US tax forms (W-8BEN-E) before the first invoice is paid. This is general practice; I found no survey on it.

### Pilots

- Poll data quoted in SaaS circles: 42 percent of companies say their free pilots convert to paid, against 74 percent for paid pilots ([SaaStr](https://www.saastr.com/what-is-the-typical-conversion-from-paid-pilot-to-annual-contract-in-b2b-saas-2), [Startup Strategies](https://startupstrategies.substack.com/p/pilots-should-they-be-free-or-paid); poll and vendor data, date unclear, search summary).
- The same sources say free pilots make sense only when the market is thousands of small buyers.

### The benefits calendar

- **Planning starts early.** A January 1 plan year enrolls in October and November, and planning starts 90 to 120 days before the plan year ([Hotaling Insurance](https://hotalinginsurance.com/commercial/employee-benefits-open-enrollment-guide-2), 2026).
- **The 2027 decisions are already made.** The Business Group on Health surveyed employers on 2027 in June 2026 (127 employers, 11 million covered lives; released August 2026) ([BGH press release](https://www.businessgrouphealth.org/newsroom/news-and-press-releases/press-releases/2027-employer-healthcare-strategy-survey), [PLANADVISER](https://www.planadviser.com/2027-employer-healthcare-costs-projected-to-grow-up-to-11/)).
- **What that means:** the 2027 benefits line is closed to new vendors. The next open window is spring and summer 2027, deciding the 2028 plan year, unless an employer runs an off-cycle plan year.
- **What can still be bought in Q4 2026:**
  - Event and ERG money:
    - Diwali, Sunday 2026-11-08, with the festival running November 6 to 10 ([Farmers' Almanac](https://www.farmersalmanac.com/when-is-diwali)).
    - International Men's Day, November 19.
    - Holiday-season sessions.
  - Learning budgets.
  - Year-end unspent budgets.
  - Budgets for the May 2027 observances, which are set in Q4 and Q1. The observances are AAPI Heritage Month (US), Asian Heritage Month (Canada, [Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/canadian-heritage/campaigns/asian-heritage-month.html)) and Mental Health Awareness Month (US).
- **Lead times.** Guides recommend starting heritage-month planning 4 to 6 weeks ahead for a speaker, and 3 to 6 months ahead for larger events ([Verbate](https://www.verbate.io/knowledge-center/verbate-guide-how-to-create-plan-and-track-successful-erg-events), [Aurum Bureau](https://aurumbureau.com/blog/how-far-in-advance-book-keynote-speaker/), search summary).

### Cold outreach is legal in both countries, with rules

- **US email.** CAN-SPAM allows unsolicited business email that identifies the sender, has an honest subject line and honours opt-outs ([FTC compliance guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business)).
- **Canada email.** CASL covers B2B email ([CRTC FAQ](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/faq500.htm)). Consent is implied if all three of these hold:
  - the address was conspicuously published (a website or directory);
  - it carries no "no solicitation" note;
  - the message is relevant to the person's role.
  Unsubscribes must be honoured within 10 business days ([mailtester](https://mailtester.com/blog/casl-rules-for-cold-b2b-email-to-canadian-prospects/), 2026).
- **Canada calls.** The National Do Not Call List rules do not apply to calls to businesses. Callers must still register as telemarketers (free) and keep an internal do-not-call list for 3 years and 14 days ([DNCL exemptions](https://www.lnnte-dncl.gc.ca/en/Organization/Exemptions); [lawyerinfo.ca](https://lawyerinfo.ca/guides/ontario/business-law-ontario/legal-rules-for-b2b-telemarketing-and-the-national-do-not-call-list-dncl-in-canada/)).
- **Reply-rate benchmarks** (vendor, 2026):
  - The average B2B cold email reply rate is 3.43 percent in 2026, down from about 5 percent in 2025 and 8.5 percent in 2019 ([Instantly](https://instantly.ai/cold-email-benchmark-report-2026), [Martal](https://martal.ca/b2b-cold-email-statistics-lb/)).
  - B2B cold calls convert about 2 to 3 percent from dial to meeting, and reaching a decision maker takes about 8 attempts ([Cleverly](https://www.cleverly.co/blog/cold-calling-statistics), [Focus Digital](https://focus-digital.co/average-cold-call-conversion-rate/)).
  - Estimate: 300 targeted emails give about 9 conversations, and 400 dials give about 10 meetings.
- **Hours from Pakistan** (PKT is UTC+5):
  - 9am to 5pm US Eastern is 6pm to 2am in Pakistan until US daylight time ends on 2026-11-01.
  - After that it is 7pm to 3am.
  - Pacific time runs 3 hours later.

## 3. Competition

### EAPs: cheap, bundled, underused

- **Price.** $1 to $3 PEPM (Oliver Wyman, Apr 2025). They are often bundled with disability or life insurance at little or no added cost.
- **Use.**
  - Median use is 5.5 percent; the industry average is 3 to 5 percent ([Meditopia](https://meditopia.com/en/forwork/articles/eap-statistics-and-utilization-rates), 2025, vendor, search summary).
  - 61 percent of US workers had EAP access in 2024 (Bureau of Labor Statistics, via Meditopia).
  - 59 percent of employers offer an EAP, and more than half of eligible employees have never used one (Prudential survey via [Insurance Business](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/benefits/mental-health-utilization-is-up--but-employer-spending-fell-7-in-2025-587992.aspx), 2026, search summary).
- **EBRI 2025** (410 decision makers at employers of 500+):
  - Engagement challenges: lack of awareness 47 percent, stigma 43 percent, confidentiality concerns 40 percent, cultural barriers 33 percent, limited budget 33 percent.
  - Culturally competent care is offered by 26 percent.
  - 85 percent want to enhance their mental health benefits, and 73 percent are considering more mental health services.
  - From the charts: 58 percent are considering wellness workshops, 37 percent see peer support groups as a way to raise engagement, and about 32 percent already offer peer support programs.

### Consolidation and point-solution fatigue

- **Business Group on Health (BGH):**
  - 51 percent of employers are changing or re-bidding health and well-being vendors, and 95 percent have issued an RFP in at least one vendor category ([BGH Trends to Watch 2026](https://www.businessgrouphealth.org/resources/trends-to-watch-in-2026), search summary).
  - "Employers will continue to streamline programs and vendors in pursuit of simplicity, robust clinical outcomes, improved employee experience and cost savings" ([BGH 2026 Employer Well-being Strategy Survey](https://www.businessgrouphealth.org/resources/2026-employer-well-being-strategy-survey-executive-summary): fielded January to February 2026, 156 employers, 12.4 million employees; search summary).
  - 94 percent of employers raised their expectations that well-being vendors deliver measurable outcomes ([BGH 2025 Well-being Strategy Survey](https://www.businessgrouphealth.org/en/Resources/2025-Employer-Well-being-Strategy-Survey-Executive-Summary), search summary).
- **Consultants.** 84 percent of benefits consultants report "solution fatigue" among clients ([Cleo](https://hicleo.com/resource/point-solution-fatigue-is-real-and-its-the-hidden-driver-of-your-excess-healthcare-spend/), vendor claim, search summary).

### Group formats already sold to employers

- **Modern Health Circles.** Live, provider-led group sessions in "workshop, discussion or listen and learn" formats, launched in April 2021 and included in the platform fee. Vendor claims, 2021 to 2022 ([Modern Health](https://www.modernhealth.com/press-media-news/modern-health-expands-care-delivery-model-with-launch-of-group-circles), search summary):
  - 250+ sessions and 6,000+ attendees.
  - About 90 employers created custom Circles.
  - 96 percent of attendees were likely to join again.
  - 50 percent were new to Modern Health, and 64 percent went on to other services.
- **Coa.** Live "emotional fitness" classes. Consumer classes cost $25 to $30 and membership $49 a month. Employer programs at Asana, Spotify and Silicon Valley Bank; employer demand rose 900 percent from March 2020 ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/therapy-startup-coa-raises-3m-to-launch-worlds-first-gym-for-mental-health-301152512.html), 2020 to 2021).
- **Keep Company.** Expert-facilitated peer groups for working parents and caregivers, matched by algorithm and sold as an employer benefit since 2022. It reported 6x year-on-year growth in April 2024 and has raised $2.3 million. Pricing is not public ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240423981114/en/On-the-Heels-of-6X-YOY-Growth-Keep-Company-Announces-First-Chief-Technology-Officer-Zvi-Band-and-%241.4M-Fundraising-Round), 2024-04-23; [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/geristengel/2024/08/28/caregiver-isolation-targeted-by-keep-company/), 2024-08-28; search summaries).
- **Circles (Tel Aviv, consumer).** Matched cohorts of 6 to 8 with professional facilitators ([Startup Intros](https://startupintros.com/orgs/circles-online-group-support)).

### Peer support companies selling to employers

- **7 Cups.**
  - Free volunteer listeners: 160,000+ in 189 countries.
  - Paid consumer tiers: $7.99 a month for "Paths", and therapy from $159 a month.
  - Custom pricing for organizations.
  - Publicly criticised for volunteer quality and safety ([ChoosingTherapy review](https://www.choosingtherapy.com/7-cups-review/), 2026; [7 Cups for organizations](https://www.7cups.com/solutions/)).
- **Supportiv, HearMe and Kindly Human.**
  - Employer pricing is not public.
  - They are largely distributed through EAPs and health plans; M&S EAP, for example, resells [Kindly Human peer support](https://www.mseap.com/service/eap/peer-support-solution/).
  - A 2025 study of digital peer support inside an EAP found 73.6 percent of 587 chats happened after business hours ([JMIR Human Factors](https://humanfactors.jmir.org/2025/1/e68221), 2025, search summary).

### Culturally specific providers

- **Anise Health** (Asian American, US-licensed clinicians).
  - It sells ERGs "psychoeducational webinars", "facilitated support groups" and "speaking events for holidays and Asian heritage month", alongside self-pay therapy ($99 intake, $125 per session).

  - South Asian women carry caregiving in multi-generational households.
  - Many avoid supports that do not feel "culturally safe".
  - 41 percent cite lack of mentorship as a career barrier, and 55 percent want sponsorship and networking designed for them.

### How buyers react

I found no survey of employer attitudes to offshore mental-health staff. The reaction shows up instead in rules and procurement terms.

- **Licensure follows the client.** A clinician must be licensed where the client is physically located ([CCHP](https://www.cchpca.org/topic/cross-state-licensing-professional-requirements/); [Holt Law](https://djholtlaw.com/crossing-state-lines-a-guide-to-teletherapy-compliance-for-therapists/), 2026).
- **The title is protected everywhere in the US.** All 50 states restrict who may represent themselves as a psychologist ([Church Law & Tax](https://www.churchlawandtax.com/pastor-church-law/liabilities-limitations-and-restrictions/state-regulation-of-psychologists-and-counselors/)). Two examples:
  - Oregon's rule covers any description of services using "psychology", "psychological" or "psychotherapy" ([ORS 675.020](https://oregon.public.law/statutes/ors_675.020); [Oregon Board FAQ](https://www.oregon.gov/psychology/Documents/Unlicensed_Practice_FAQ.pdf)).
  - New York restricts "psychologist", "psychology" and "psychological" ([NYSED](https://www.op.nysed.gov/professions/psychology/questions-answers-about-licensure-and-limited-permits)).
- **What that means for Hearth.** Pakistan-trained psychologists cannot be presented to US employees as psychologists and cannot deliver therapy to them. Coaching or peer support is the lawful lane, which is exactly how Wysa uses its India-based therapists. Before any B2B material mentions psychology credentials, get a US and Canadian legal review.
- **Canada.** Ontario's controlled act of psychotherapy applies to practitioners outside Ontario who serve Ontario clients by video ([CRPO](https://crpo.ca/apply-to-crpo/controlled-act-of-psychotherapy/)).
- **Data and where the work is done.** HIPAA does not ban offshoring on its own, but state contracts often do ([McDermott Will & Emery](https://www.mcdermottlaw.com/insights/us-healthcare-offshoring-patient-data-privacy-laws-regulations/); [National Law Review](https://natlawreview.com/article/do-you-catch-our-drift-navigating-waters-offshoring-and-patient-data)):
  - Texas bars offshore work and data in its managed care contract.
  - Ohio executive orders bar agencies from buying offshore services.
  - Florida restricts offshore storage of certain health records.
- **Employees.**
  - Confidentiality is already a top barrier: 40 percent in EBRI 2025.
  - 2 in 5 employees worry their career would suffer if they raised mental health at work ([NAMI 2025 poll](https://www.nami.org/research/publications-reports/survey-reports/the-2025-nami-workplace-mental-health-poll/), search summary).
  - Hearth's own objection list already includes "Is it safe to say this to a company in Pakistan?"
- **Where offshore delivery does sell.**
  - Multinationals buying care for staff in the provider's own country (MindNation, Intellect, MindFi).
  - Text coaching (Wysa).
  - An adjacent opening, outside this question: North American companies with teams in Pakistan, where Pakistani psychologists practise under local rules.

## 4. Climate

### United States: DEI and ERGs since January 2025

- **ERGs must be open to all.** On 2025-03-19 the EEOC and DOJ issued technical assistance saying that restricting ERG membership to a protected class is "unlawful segregation" ([Littler](https://www.littler.com/news-analysis/asap/eeoc-and-department-justice-issue-technical-assistance-dei); [Mintz](https://www.mintz.com/insights-center/viewpoints/55026/2025-03-20-eeoc-doj-release-guidelines-dei-related-discrimination)). Any ERG session must be open to all employees and organised by topic, not by ethnicity. The EEOC's DEI scrutiny continues in 2026 ([KMK Law](https://www.kmklaw.com/labor-employment/the-eeocs-renewed-focus-on-employer-dei-programs-in-2026)).
- **Company moves.** Amazon phased out funding for ERGs. Disney and Molson Coors rebranded their ERGs as business groups ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/01/16/dei-rollback-companies-amazon-meta-mcdonalds), 2025-01-16; [Raconteur](https://www.raconteur.net/talent-culture/dei-rollback-listicle)).
- **Budgets.**
  - 8 percent of companies are reducing DEI spend, 65 percent are unchanged, and 22 percent are increasing (Resume.org via [Piktochart](https://piktochart.com/blog/research-dei-statistics/), updated May 2026).
  - Corporate sponsorship of Pride 2026 and Juneteenth events fell ([political.org](https://political.org/2026/05/30/pride-month-corporate-sponsorships-decline-as-companies-retreat-from-lgbtq-public-support/), 2026-05-30).

### Canada

- **DEI mostly holding.** From the [Benefits Canada 2025 Future of Work Survey](https://www.benefitscanada.com/news/bencan/2025-future-of-work-summit-navigating-an-uncertain-dei-landscape/), reported 2026-01-20:
  - 78 percent of employers have a documented or in-progress DEI strategy, up from 65 percent in 2024.
  - 23 percent reduced or eliminated DEI programs, and 19 percent rebranded them.
  - The share of employees who say a DEI focus matters fell to 63 percent, from 72 percent.
- **Worker mental health is stuck at "near crisis"** ([TELUS Mental Health Index](https://www.thesafetymag.com/ca/news/general/canadas-worker-mental-health-stuck-at-near-crisis-level-telus-report-finds/547511), 2026, search summary):
  - The index scored 63.1 in Q1 2026.
  - 34 percent of workers are at high risk, against 14 percent in 2017 to 2019.
  - 32 percent feel isolated, and 27 percent feel unsafe disclosing to managers.
- **Coverage is thin.** 47 percent of employers with mental health coverage cap it below $1,000 a year (Benefits Canada Healthcare Survey 2025 via [Benefits Canada](https://www.benefitscanada.com/benefits/sounding-board-how-to-close-the-mental-health-benefits-utilization-gap-in-canadian-workplaces/), search summary).

### Is employer mental health spending rising?

- **Costs.**
  - Benefit costs are projected to rise 8.2 percent per employee in 2027, the largest increase since 2003, and 48 percent of large employers will shift more cost to employees ([Mercer preliminary](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/payers/employers-health-benefits-costs-could-rise-82-2027-marsh-survey), September 2026).
  - BGH projects a median 2027 trend of 9.2 percent, or 8 percent after plan changes (June 2026, 127 employers).
  - The 2026 trend was 9 percent according to BGH ([press release](https://www.businessgrouphealth.org/newsroom/news-and-press-releases/press-releases/2026-employer-health-care-strategy-survey); June to July 2025, 121 employers) and 6.5 percent according to Mercer, the highest since 2010 ([Mercer](https://www.mercer.com/en-us/about/newsroom/employers-are-bracing-for-the-highest-health-benefit-cost-increase-in-15-years/)).
- **Priority.** Cost is the most important factor in benefits decisions for 54 percent of employers, up from 38 percent in 2025; attracting and retaining talent fell to 19 percent ([Lockton 2026 National Benefits Survey](https://lockbox.lockton.com/asset/f1556c85-7371-47b4-9b5d-786bc086d2ee/2026-Lockton-National-Benefits-Survey-Executive-Summary.pdf): 1,705 responses, January to February 2026).
- **Mental health in particular.**
  - Use is rising: 73 percent of large employers saw increases (BGH).
  - Average spending fell about 7 percent in 2025 (NFP 2026 US Benefits Trend Report, via [Insurance Business](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/benefits/mental-health-utilization-is-up--but-employer-spending-fell-7-in-2025-587992.aspx), 2026).
  - Mercer's 2026 survey: about two-thirds of large employers prioritise access to behavioral care, about three-quarters offer digital stress or resilience tools, and more than half plan live or in-person stress resources.

Net: demand is rising and employers say they want to add support, but the money is tightening and flowing towards consolidation and measurable outcomes.

## 5. Workplace group sessions: what works

- **Comfort with coworkers is partial** ([NAMI 2025](https://www.nami.org/research/publications-reports/survey-reports/the-2025-nami-workplace-mental-health-poll/), search summary):
  - 61 percent of employees are comfortable discussing mental health at work.
  - 83 percent are comfortable with a colleague they consider a friend, and 57 percent with their manager.
  - 2 in 5 worry about harm to their career.
  - In Canada, 27 percent of workers feel unsafe disclosing to managers (TELUS 2026).
- **What vendors actually run:**
  - Topic sessions in workshop, discussion or "listen and learn" formats, open across employers, plus custom sessions for one employer (Modern Health Circles).
  - Therapist-led circles of about 20 inside an ERG (Anise at Accenture).
  - Small matched peer groups of parents or caregivers with an expert facilitator (Keep Company).
  - Matched cohorts of 6 to 8 (Circles, consumer).
- **Participation in employer-run social activities is under half:** 47 percent overall and 43 percent for Gen Z ([Reward Gateway](https://www.rewardgateway.com/blog/workplace-loneliness-new-research-for-2025-rger), 2025, search summary; not verified).
- **Evidence gap.** I found no rigorous public data on attendance at disclosure circles among coworkers. The confidentiality numbers point to education-first topic sessions with optional, light sharing inside a company, and to running any disclosure-based circle across companies or anonymously.

## 6. Does "affordable" win, and how do small vendors land their first 3 to 10 employers?

- **Price matters more than ever, but not as "cheaper".**
  - 54 percent of employers say cost comes first (Lockton 2026). But the test is total cost, simplicity and outcomes, not a cheaper extra vendor on top of the stack (BGH: "simplicity, robust clinical outcomes, improved employee experience and cost savings"; 94 percent demand measurable outcomes).
  - The incumbent EAP already costs $1 to $3 PEPM or comes bundled at no cost, so "cheaper" does not differentiate. The gaps are low use (3 to 5 percent) and cultural fit (26 percent coverage).
- **Hearth's own rule conflicts with the pitch.** An "affordable prices" B2B pitch contradicts the positioning rule in `.agents/product-marketing.md` ("premium and craft-led, never the cheaper alternative").
- **What gets small vendors their first customers:**
  - **Founder-led selling until the pitch repeats.** The CEO makes the first sales; "Do not hire sales people until you're feeling like you can teach someone else what works" ([Rock Health](https://rockhealth.com/insights/streamlining-enterprise-sales-in-digital-health/); [Halle Tecco](https://halletecco.substack.com/p/four-ways-to-land-your-first-health); search summary).
  - **An ERG-first way into large companies.** Anise Health's route ran from webinar, to circles, to a one-year ERG contract (Accenture, McKinsey, JPMorgan groups).
  - **B2C2B.** Follow existing users into their employers, and go to enterprise once "repeatable usage patterns" exist ([a16z](https://a16z.com/b2c2b-in-digital-health-a-founders-playbook/), 2021-12-01). Hearth's paying members' employers are its warmest leads.
  - **Paid pilots over free ones** (section 2).
  - **Money employees spend themselves.** LSAs and stipends where wellness is eligible (Compt 2026, vendor).

## 7. The three findings that matter most for a tiny, bootstrapped, culturally specific peer-support startup

1. **Against B2B-first through benefits: that door is shut for 2027 and getting narrower.**
   - Costs are rising 8.2 to 9.2 percent in 2027, the largest increase since 2003.
   - Cost is the top criterion for 54 percent of employers.
   - 51 percent are re-bidding or changing well-being vendors.
   - Mental health spending fell about 7 percent in 2025.
   - The incumbent EAP costs $1 to $3 PEPM or nothing.
   - The earliest real window is spring and summer 2027, and it runs through brokers, security reviews and outcomes data Hearth does not have. The public example drew nine bidders, including Optum, Lyra, Spring Health and TELUS, for a five-year contract.
2. **Against B2B-first through benefits: compliance and the offshore question.**
   - Licensure follows the employee, the title "psychologist" is protected in all 50 states, and Ontario regulates out-of-province psychotherapy.
   - Enterprise buyers ask for SOC 2 or HITRUST and a BAA, and public-sector contracts often bar offshore work.
   - Offshore support sells today either as coaching (Wysa's India-based therapists) or to staff in the provider's own country.
   - So Hearth's Keepers must be sold as trained peer supporters, not psychologists. That is weaker in a benefits RFP but fine for workshops and small companies.
3. **For a narrow B2B wedge: fast, small, discretionary money meets a documented gap.**
   - ERG and event budgets are holding (74 percent steady, 15 percent up in 2026), and virtual sessions sell for $1,000 to $2,500.
   - Only 26 percent of large employers offer culturally competent care, and 58 percent are considering wellness workshops.
   - South Asian employees in Canada are asking for "culturally safe" supports (May 2026).
   - Anise Health turned exactly this into a year-long Accenture ERG contract.
   - Cold email and calls to named ERG and People leads are legal in both countries. Expect about 3 percent replies.
   - The US condition: sessions must be open to all employees and organised by topic (caregiving, family expectations, immigrant parents), not restricted by ethnicity.
