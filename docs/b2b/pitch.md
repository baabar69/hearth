# Hearth for Teams: the pitch

Written 2026-09-11 for owners of companies with 10 to 100 staff in the US and Canada. It is results-first, and every results claim is true today, sourced, or measured (see "The rules behind the words" at the end). Fill in anything in [brackets]. A lawyer approves the final wording before cold calls start; warm conversations can use it now.

**The one idea running through every line:** give every person on your team their own coach. Coaching is usually an executive perk, at about $272 an hour in North America (ICF, 2023). Hearth gives it to everyone on the team for $59 a month.

**Two offer changes this copy assumes (founder decisions):**
- The first 60-minute team session is free. It is the demo: people meet a coach, and only the ones who want one sign up. Extra sessions stay at $450.
- An optional guarantee: "If your team's wellbeing score hasn't moved after three months, your fourth month is free." Only use it if you adopt it.

**One rule change:**
- The $272 comparison is a price comparison. The consumer rule says price is never the argument, so this is for B2B material only.

## The one-pager

**Give every person on your team their own coach.**

The one-to-one coaching executives get, for everyone who wants it: the same trained coach every two weeks, for work pressure and whatever your people carry from home.

### Why owners do it

- **Your people carry more than their jobs.** A parent's illness overseas, a new baby, money, a marriage. It comes to work with them.
- **You can't be everyone's coach.** You're already the boss and the HR department.
- **Coaching has the research.** Studies of workplace coaching find it improves wellbeing, coping and performance.

### What each person gets

- Their own coach, matched by hand within 72 hours. The same one every time.
- A 35 to 60 minute call every two weeks, by video or phone.
- Messages in between, answered within a day.
- A short note from their coach every Friday.
- A coach who understands family and culture, in English, Urdu, Hindi or Punjabi. *(Confirm what each coach speaks.)*

### What you get

- **A wellbeing score for your team every quarter,** from a five-question check (the WHO-5). Never who said what.
- **Nothing to roll out:** one invoice and no app. Staff sign up in 12 minutes.
- **A coach for yourself,** if you want one: $99 a month.

### How it works

1. **A free 60-minute team session.** It's open to everyone, and nobody has to share anything.
2. **People who want a coach sign up.** You pay only for them: $59 a month each, three people minimum, three months to start.
3. **Every quarter, you see the team's wellbeing score.**

### What it isn't

- **Not therapy.** Coaches don't diagnose or treat. If someone needs a therapist, their coach helps them find one.
- **Not a crisis line.** In a crisis, call or text 988 (US and Canada).

|---|---|
| "We already have an EAP." | Great, keep it. An EAP is a number to call when something is wrong, and more than half of employees who have one have never used it (Prudential, 2026). A coach is someone they already know, every two weeks, whether or not something is wrong. |
| "We can't afford it." | You only pay for the people who sign up. Three people is $177 a month. Start there, watch the wellbeing score, and decide in three months. |
| "My people won't use it." | Then it costs you almost nothing. That's what the free session is for: they meet a coach, and only the ones who want one sign up. You're not buying forty licences nobody opens. |
| "Is this therapy?" | No. It's coaching for work and life: what matters, what's in the way, and the next step. If someone needs a therapist, their coach helps them find one. |
| "Who are the coaches?" | Trained coaches, matched by hand for language, culture and time zone. We're based in Pakistan and say so up front. That's how every person on your team gets a coach for $59 a month, when coaching in North America averages $272 an hour. |
| "How do I know it works?" | Two ways. The research: studies of workplace coaching find it improves wellbeing, coping and performance. And your own numbers: a five-question wellbeing check when people start and every quarter. If the score doesn't move, don't renew. |
| "What will I see?" | Numbers, never names or conversations: how many people use it, and the team's wellbeing score. That's in the agreement you sign. |
| "What if someone's in crisis?" | A coach isn't a crisis service and says so. If someone is in danger, their coach gives them 988 or 911 and stays with them while they reach out, and we follow up. |
| "Send me some info." | Happy to. So I send the right thing: how many people are on your team, and who hears their problems now? |

## The rules behind the words

**Say now:** "their own coach", "the one-to-one coaching executives get", "the same coach every two weeks", "you only pay for the people who sign up", "you see the team's wellbeing score, never who said what".

**Say with the source (always name it):**
- "Coaching usually costs about $272 an hour in North America" (ICF Global Coaching Study, 2023: $272 in North America, $244 worldwide).
- "Research on workplace coaching finds it improves wellbeing, coping and performance" (Theeboom, Beersma and van Vianen, 2014; Jones, Woods and Guillaume, 2016).
- "More than half of employees with an EAP have never used it" (Prudential, 2026, via Insurance Business).
- The research only applies if Keepers actually coach. In every call, keep the same shape: what matters, what's in the way, and one next step.

**Earn with your own numbers:**
- From about January, after 12 weeks of WHO-5 data, you can say "wellbeing scores rose from X to Y (n people)".
- Say "improves performance" about Hearth itself only once something about work is measured.

**Never:**
- treats, cures, fixes, proven
- therapy, psychologist, counselling, mental health treatment
- the names of conditions
- reduces turnover by X%, guaranteed ROI
- licensed, certified coach (until ICF-certified)
- that Hearth is an EAP
- HIPAA-compliant, 100% confidential, crisis support
- affordable, cheaper

Prefer "wellbeing" to "mental health". An implied claim counts the same as a stated one, and misleading claims on calls to businesses have been banned since May 2024.
