## Three conclusions

**1. Google has us classified under the wrong topic.** Not one impression, in three
months, for peer support, emotional support, grief, therapy alternatives, or anything
in the category we actually operate in. The domain reads to a classifier as being
about "hearth" the English noun.

**2. Nobody searches the brand.** Zero impressions for "dearhearth" or "dear hearth".
This reframes the original complaint completely: the site does not fail to rank for
its brand name — the brand name has no search demand yet. That is a marketing
problem, not an SEO one, and no amount of technical work touches it.

**3. The name is an active liability.** Two of our impressions came from people
looking for *other counselling practices* called Hearth. We also compete with the
dictionary, and with Hearth & Hand with Magnolia. Permanent tax, not fixable.

**One thing that is working**: the best-performing page on the site is a Keeper
profile. A page about a named human produced the only click of the quarter. That is
direct evidence for the E-E-A-T argument — and it raises a question that needs
answering first (KAN-45).

## What to target, and what to leave alone

### Do not bother

| Query | Why not |
|---|---|
| `therapy`, `online therapy` | BetterHelp, Talkspace, Psychology Today, major health publishers. Years of authority ahead of us. |
| `betterhelp` | Their own brand term. |
| `hearth` | Common noun, plus several established businesses. Unwinnable, permanently. |
| `mental health` | Among the most competitive informational spaces on the web, and heavily YMYL-filtered. |

Chasing any of these burns effort for nothing. Say so plainly to anyone who suggests
otherwise.

### Hard but worth attempting

`betterhelp alternatives`, `cheaper than betterhelp`, `7 cups alternatives`

The SERP is owned by affiliate listicles — calmsage, therapyhelpers,
onlinetherapymantra, innercalmguide, meditopia. Google prefers apparently-independent
reviewers for "alternatives" queries, so our own page will struggle.

**The faster route is to get listed inside those articles** rather than to outrank
them (KAN-39). They already rank, they are already read by our exact audience, and
their authors want new services to cover. Outreach, not SEO.

### Genuinely winnable

Long-tail informational queries where intent matches and competition is thinner:

- `what is peer support`, `peer support meaning`
- `peer support vs therapy` — we already have a page
- `someone to talk to who isn't a therapist`
- `i just want someone to talk to`
- `grief support without therapy`
- `what is a hearth keeper` — **we already get impressions for this**

That last one is the most interesting item on the list. The demand exists, we are
already half-visible for it, and "keeper of the hearth" is precisely the metaphor the
product is built on. Answer the question honestly, then bridge. See KAN-41.

### The brand token

"Hearth" is unwinnable. **"Dear Hearth" is effectively unique** — no competition, and
it matches the domain. Nobody searches it yet, which is exactly the problem worth
solving, because it is the one brand term we could own outright.

Everywhere outside the site, teach "Dear Hearth" rather than bare "Hearth". Tracked
as KAN-46.

## The content cluster

Hub and spoke. One pillar, several spokes, all interlinked. Search engines infer a
site's topic from the body of content it hosts and how that content links together;
ten substantive interlinked pages on peer support move the classifier further than a
hundred links to a thin one.

| Page | Targets | Ticket |
|---|---|---|
| Pillar: What is peer support? | `what is peer support` | KAN-40 |
| What is a Hearth Keeper? | `hearthkeeper`, `keeper of the hearth` | KAN-41 |
| Someone to talk to who isn't a therapist | `i just want someone to talk to` | KAN-42 |
| BetterHelp / Talkspace / 7 Cups comparison | `betterhelp alternatives` | KAN-43 |
| Grief support when you don't want therapy | `grief support without therapy` | KAN-44 |

Existing pages `/hearth-vs-therapy` and `/learn/peer-support-vs-therapy` are already
the right shape and should be linked into the cluster rather than duplicated.

## Rules for every page

- **1,200 words minimum.** Thin content actively hurts a YMYL site.
- **Title under 51 characters** — the layout appends `· Hearth`, which is 9 more.
- **WebPage + BreadcrumbList schema**, plus Article schema on guides.
- **Interlink** to the pillar and at least two siblings, in both directions.
- **Never imply clinical outcomes.** Hearth is peer support, not therapy. That line
  is positioning, compliance boundary, and relevance signal simultaneously.
- **Say where competitors are better.** 7 Cups is free. BetterHelp has licensed
  clinicians. A comparison concluding we win everything convinces nobody, and honesty
  is what makes other sites willing to cite us.
- **Crisis resources near the top** on anything touching grief or distress. Someone
  may arrive in a worse state than the query suggests.
- **Would this help someone who never buys?** If a section only exists to sell, cut
  it.

## Timeframe

Six to twelve months before topical authority meaningfully shifts. There is no fast
version of this, and anyone offering one is selling something.

**The first measurable sign of progress** is a single impression for any query
containing "peer support". That count is currently zero, so any movement at all is
signal.
