# SERP research: alternatives to therapy, cheap therapy (2026-10-24)

Why this exists: the founder wants Hearth to rank for "best alternative to therapy" and
"cheap therapy". Founder rule: therapy wording is fine in titles and headings, but Hearth is
never called therapy and a Keeper is never called a therapist.

Method: Google autocomplete (US, CA, UK, a–z expansions). Google blocks scripted results
pages, so top results were read through Brave Search (set to US) and WebSearch: close to
Google, not exact. Facts verified 2026-10-24.

## Family A: alternatives to therapy

- **Real phrasing:** "alternatives to therapy" is strongest. Variants: for depression,
  anxiety, men, trauma; "when you can't afford it"; "what can i do instead of therapy";
  "chatgpt instead of therapy"; "coaching instead of therapy"; "betterhelp alternatives".
- **Avoid:** "best alternative to therapy" is not a real query; Google rewrites it to
  "best alternative therapy" (acupuncture). "Alternatives to talk therapy" means other
  clinical treatments.
- **Who ranks:** Talkspace blog (strong), Citizen Advocates, Verywell Health, Mind UK, GQ,
  LA Times (2021), thin blogs (hopefulpanda, wojdo, breeze), treatment centres, and two Reddit
  threads in the top 5 (a sign Google lacks a page that fully answers it). Beatable.
- **BetterHelp alternatives:** Forbes Health, Verywell Mind, HelpGuide, affiliates. Hard;
  `/hearth-vs-betterhelp` is enough for now.
- **Gaps competitors leave:** warmlines, one-to-one peer support, coaching, AI chatbots,
  "same person over time", price per option, an honest "therapy is the answer when" section.
- **Built:** `/learn/alternatives-to-therapy`.

## Family B: cheap therapy

- **Head terms** (cheap/affordable/low cost/free therapy, near me): owned by Open Path's
  homepage, Talkspace, local directories, Reddit. Not winnable for a non-therapy site.
- **Guide terms (winnable):** cheap therapy options, affordable therapy options, cheapest
  way to get therapy, cheap therapy online, cheapest online therapy. Ranked by Healthline,
  HelpGuide, GoodRx, CNET, US News, platform blogs, and weak or spam pages.
- **Built:** `/learn/cheap-therapy`. `/learn/cant-afford-therapy` narrowed to the
  step-by-step "can't afford therapy but need it". `/learn/therapy-without-insurance` kept
  for "affordable therapy without insurance".
- **Extra opening:** "can i pay someone to listen to me" has thin results. Built
  `/learn/pay-someone-to-listen`.

## Verified facts (checked 2026-10-24)

1. Open Path: US $40–70 individual, $40–80 couples/family, $30 student interns, $65 one-time membership. Canada C$50–90, C$50–100, C$40, C$89 membership.
2. BetterHelp: "$70 to $100 per week (billed every 4 weeks)" (betterhelp.com/faq).
3. Talkspace: out of pocket per week $69 messaging, $99 video plus messaging, $109 with workshops; "most insured members have a $0 copay".
4. Online-Therapy.com: $60, $90 or $120 a week.
5. Zhu et al., Health Affairs Scholar 2024 (PMC11412241): cash-pay sessions averaged $143.26 vs $82.77 under Medicaid; about a third of private-practice therapists do not take insurance.
6. SimplePractice 2023–24: state averages $122 (Missouri) to $227 (North Dakota).
7. 988 (US): call, text or chat, free, 24/7. 988 (Canada): call or text, 24/7.
8. SAMHSA National Helpline 1-800-662-4357: free referrals, 24/7, English and Spanish; FindTreatment.gov.
9. CCBHCs must serve anyone regardless of ability to pay; 500+ clinics (July 2025).
10. HRSA health centres: no one denied for inability to pay; full discount at or below 100% of poverty guidelines.
11. NAMI Connection: free, peer-led, 90 minutes, adults 18+, many online.
12. Warmlines: free, peer-staffed, about 30 states (APA).
13. NHS Talking Therapies: free self-referral in England, 18+.
14. Ontario Structured Psychotherapy: free short-term CBT, self-referral.
15. Wellness Together Canada closed 3 April 2024 (CBC). Many competitor pages still list it.
16. The old unsourced "$165 national average" on our cost page was replaced with items 5 and 6.
