# SERP research: "peer support" (2026-10-24)

Why this exists: the founder wants Hearth ranking for "peer support". This is what the
results page looked like before the October guides went live, and what it would take.

Method: real Google results loaded in the founder's Chrome with `gl=us&hl=en&pws=0`
(the IP is Lahore, so results are close to US, not city-exact). Google autocomplete for
US, CA and UK. No paid tools, so no search volumes or domain authority.

## Verdict

- **Top 100 for "peer support":** realistic in about 3–6 months with a cited pillar page
  and 5–15 relevant sites linking to Hearth.
- **Top 10:** a 12-month-plus effort. The top 10 is held by institutional authority, not
  page quality.
- **Fastest wins:** `/learn/peer-support-vs-therapy` (already #13 on 2026-10-24), then the
  one-to-one and definition clusters.

## Verified citations (checked against PubMed or the source)

1. Mead, Hilton, Curtis. Peer support: a theoretical perspective. Psychiatr Rehabil J 2001;25(2):134-41. doi:10.1037/h0095032
2. Solomon. Peer support/peer provided services. Psychiatr Rehabil J 2004;27(4):392-401
3. White et al. Effectiveness of one-to-one peer support in mental health services. BMC Psychiatry 2020;20:534. PMC7657356. 19 trials, n=3,329; modest gains in self-reported recovery and empowerment; no effect on clinical symptoms or service use
4. Cooper et al. Umbrella review of peer support. BMC Med 2024;22:72. PMC10902990
5. Smit et al. Psychol Med 2023;53(11):5332-41. PMC10476060. 28 RCTs, small effects (clinical recovery g=0.19, personal recovery g=0.15)
6. Pfeiffer et al. Gen Hosp Psychiatry 2011;33(1):29-36. Peer support beat usual care for depressive symptoms; no significant difference from group CBT
7. Lloyd-Evans et al. BMC Psychiatry 2014;14:39. Severe mental illness: little effect on hospitalisation or symptoms; some on hope, recovery, empowerment
8. Chien et al. Cochrane 2019, CD010880.pub2 (schizophrenia, insufficient evidence)
9. Dale et al. Cochrane 2008, CD006903.pub2 (peer support telephone calls)
10. Kahlon et al. JAMA Psychiatry 2021;78(6):616-22. PMC7903319. Lay callers, 4 weeks: lower loneliness, depression and anxiety
11. Bartone et al. Omega 2019;80(1):137-66 (bereavement peer support)
12. Holt-Lunstad et al. Perspect Psychol Sci 2015;10(2):227-37 (70 studies; loneliness 26% higher likelihood of death)
13. SAMHSA, Value of Peers 2017: the peer role "complements, but does not duplicate or replace" therapists (PDF returns 403 to scripts)
