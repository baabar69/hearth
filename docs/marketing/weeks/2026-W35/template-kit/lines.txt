HEARTH · WEEK 35 · TEMPLATE DROP-IN KIT
Use these to fill any Reel template (CapCut, Edits, Canva). One line per text slot, in order. Never add an em dash. If the template has fewer slots, cut from the middle, keep the first and last two lines.

TITLE / HOOK
a letter to the eldest daughter

BODY LINES (in order)
you take the sunday call in the car, engine off.
you wipe the counter at 11pm. it was already clean.
you danced at the wedding. you cried on the drive home.
nobody asks you the question you ask everyone else.
there should be one person whose only job is to ask you back.

CLOSER (last slot)
hearth · one trained listener, yours for years

CAPTION (paste under the Reel)
If you are the eldest daughter, you already know the Sunday call is a shift you clock into.
You take the question about money, the question about her knee, the question about when you are coming home.
Then you hang up and start your own evening at 9pm.
Hearth is peer support, not therapy. One trained listener, yours for years.
Send this to the person who is always the strong one.
