## Caption

Being the eldest daughter in a South Asian family is a job nobody ever wrote an ending for.

You take the Sunday call. You do the paperwork. You answer the aunt's question kindly, twice.

Then someone tells you how strong you are, and the conversation closes.

Save this for the next Sunday the phone rings, or send it to the one who never says she is tired.

If you read this twice, you already know why.
