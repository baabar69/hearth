## Production note

Cream paper (#F2EDE5) with the grain PNG at 7% multiply. No skeuomorphic phone UI anywhere: a call-log screenshot would read as borrowed and risk the originality score, so the log is implied by typography only. Each timestamp is JetBrains Mono 500, 24px, sage, uppercase, 0.18em tracking, above a Fraunces 400 line at 56 to 64px. The one original device: each timestamp stays on screen after its beat at 20% ink opacity, so by beat 5 there is a visible column of four entries. Beat 6 clears the column over 12 frames; beats 6 and 7 sit alone on empty paper. Beat 7 in ember, everything else in ink. The clipped ledger fragments in beats 2 to 5 are deliberate and make the two full sentences at the end land softer. Loop frame 539 back to frame 0 on blank paper.

Closest template: `docs/marketing/remotion-prompts/02-instagram-reel-letter-to-pattern.md` (same 1080x1920, 30fps, cream paper, Fraunces plus JetBrains Mono, ember pivot, wordmark sign-off). Deviations: 540 frames instead of 510; add a persistent TimestampStack component that beat 6 clears; ledger beats run 66 frames, under that file's 75-frame minimum, intentionally, because this lane optimises for sends and completion.

Audio: no voiceover. Silence for the first three seconds. One low wooden tick at 0.18 volume on each timestamp reveal (frames 90, 156, 222, 288, 354), which gets the Reel tagged as Original Audio. Optional piano pad fading in at beat 6 at -22dB and out by the wordmark. No trending pop audio.
