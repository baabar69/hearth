## Caption

If you are the eldest daughter, you already know the Sunday call is a shift you clock into.

You take the question about money, the question about her knee, the question about when you are coming home.

Then you hang up and start your own evening at 9pm.

Hearth is peer support, not therapy. One trained listener, yours for years.

Send this to the person who is always the strong one.

## Production note

Template: `docs/marketing/remotion-prompts/02-instagram-reel-letter-to-pattern.md`, registered as `ReelLetterToEldestDaughterSundayCall`. Deltas: 8 sequences and 750 frames (not 7 and 510); mono eyebrow counts "letter 1/6" to "letter 6/6" across beats 1 to 6, then drops; the closer sits after the wordmark in small mono so the piece ends as stationery, not as an ad; cover still at frame 60.

Audio: no voiceover. Low paper-and-room tone at about -22dB plus a near-imperceptible pencil-tick at each beat's first word reveal (frames 6, 96, 216, 336, 456, 546, 636, 696), so the Reel is tagged Original Audio. Alternative: slow solo piano (Hania Rani or Ólafur Arnalds register) at -20dB. Never a trending pop hook.

Posting: Sunday 7pm ET as a trial reel first. Watch sends per reach and US plus Canada share of reach.

Writer's notes for the founder: `#desi` was left out because it indexes to audiences inside South Asia; the closer is set small so the Reel still reads as a letter.
