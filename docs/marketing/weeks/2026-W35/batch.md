## The five hooks, and their trial alternates

1. A letter to the eldest daughter who never gets asked. (Trial: "…who takes the Sunday call" / "…nobody thinks to ask")
2. My mother calls me first, then my brother, then my aunt. (Trial: "I keep three versions of this story. One is true." / "Everyone who loves me has only heard the short version.")
3. Things nobody tells the eldest daughter. (Alt covers: "Nobody asks the eldest daughter how she is." / "You have been the strong one since you were nine.")
4. POV: everyone in your phone has called you this week. (Trial: "POV: it is 11pm and you are still the strong one." / "Your phone is full of people who need you.")
5. You said "it's fine" four times on Tuesday's call. (Trial: "I counted. You said it's fine four times on Tuesday." / "Four times on Tuesday's call, you said it was fine.")
