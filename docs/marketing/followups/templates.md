# Follow-up templates and cadence

Human-written pool, in Hearth's voice. Only `{firstName}` and one fuzzy variable per message are filled by the agent. Keep every message to three short lines. Sign with the founder's first name. No em dashes, no exclamation marks, no urgency.

## Cadence (days since last contact)

| Stage | Touches on day | Stop after |
|---|---|---|
| intake_started (did not finish) | 1, 3, 7 | 3 touches or any reply |
| intake_done_unpaid (finished the intake, no membership yet) | 1, 3, 7, 21 | 4 touches or any reply |
| paid_awaiting_match (membership paid, within the 72-hour window) | no nudges; the match email is human | |
| first_sit_booked | 1 day before the Sit, 1 day after | 2 touches |
| member, month one | 14, 30 | 2 touches |

## Pool: intake_started

1. Subject: {firstName}, no rush
   You started telling us what you are carrying and stopped partway. That is fine. It is still there when you want it, and nobody reads it until you finish.
2. Subject: the form is still open
   Twelve minutes is a lot to ask on a busy week. If it helps, the only part that matters is the last box, the one where you say it in your own words.
3. Subject: one question, {firstName}
   Was there a question on the form that put you off? Reply and tell me which one. I would rather fix the form than lose you to it.

## Pool: intake_done_unpaid

1. Subject: {firstName}, we read it
   A person read what you wrote. The one step left is choosing a membership, and within 72 hours of that we match you by hand. Nothing is decided until you say so.
2. Subject: what the first call is like
   Thirty-five minutes, video or just audio, no homework. Your Keeper will have read what you wrote, so you will not be starting from the beginning.
3. Subject: {firstName}, a small thing I noticed
   You wrote {fuzzyOneLineFromTheirIntake}. That is exactly the kind of thing a Keeper is for. The first call is the easiest place to say it out loud.
4. Subject: still here
   If the timing is wrong, your intake keeps. Come back in a month and it will still be the starting point. If Hearth is wrong for you, tell me that too. Either answer is fine.

## Pool: first_sit_booked

1. Subject: tomorrow at {time}
   Nothing to prepare. Bring whatever the week left you with, or nothing. Your Keeper will start.
2. Subject: how was it
   One line is enough. If anything about the fit felt wrong, say so now and we will assign someone else at no charge.

## Pool: member, month one

1. Subject: two weeks in
   Quick check from a person, not a survey: is the chat thread getting answered within a day? If not, I want to know.
2. Subject: your Friday notes
   You have had a few Friday notes now. If one of them landed, it would help us to know which, and why. Reply in a sentence.

## Rules
- Never send two touches in one day. Never repeat the template last used for the same person.
- Any reply that says stop, or reads as distress, ends the sequence and goes to a person.
- Price appears only if the person asks, except in the intake_done_unpaid pool where the membership step is the point; even there, state it once and plainly.
