3. **Notes-app card** (business-account unrestricted, week of Aug 20). Our variant A is this format.
4. **"Kinda chic" affirmation list** (Aug 5 to 14): one line per card, muted palette; a language framework, not audio-locked; carousel candidate.
5. **Whisper voiceover over b-roll**: 2026's close-mic intimate narration; our Tuesday lane already is this; keep.
6. **Hyper-specific POV text** on a plain frame ("POV: the eldest daughter finally lets someone take care of her"). Low production is outperforming polish.
7. **Text-message reveal** (typed chat, staggered bubbles, 20 to 40s): highest-completion structure; fits "the text I never sent my mother" with tenderness, not snark.
8. **Photo dump or scrapbook carousel with one text card**; music-backed carousels regained Reels-tab distribution in June; the week's biggest over-performer (439x) was a scrapbook carousel.
9. **Silent vlog with caption text** (pen on paper, pouring tea): muted video is a stated 2026 platform focus.
10. **Kinetic typography quote reels** under 15s, loop-edited; 2 to 5x saves in self-help niches. Our variant B is this format.

## Audio rules for a Business account (this corrects earlier guidance)

Business accounts still cannot use most licensed trending songs: only the Meta Sound Collection (about 14,000 cleared tracks), original audio, and independently licensed music. Violations get muted or reach-limited. Trending audio is a real ranking input (biggest lift in the first 24 to 48 hours), so the practical route is: trending ORIGINAL audios (Professional dashboard, trending and original tabs), Sound Collection tracks by mood, or our own named original audio. Several August lip-sync trends are creator-accounts-only; skip them. Many of the text-led formats above need no audio lock at all.

## Originality (since Apr 30, 2026)

Self-made structures (notes UI, message bubbles, POV text) are format conventions and count as original when the words, footage and execution are ours. Suppression triggers: reposted or watermarked clips, screenshots of others' posts, stitched carousels of others' work, low-effort templated AI output. Keep everything self-rendered.
