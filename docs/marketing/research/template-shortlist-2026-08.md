## Rules when using any template

1. **Audio:** CapCut templates carry their own music, usually not licensed for business accounts. Mute the template audio at export (or in Edits) and add audio inside Instagram from the trending ORIGINAL tab or the Meta Sound Collection. This is the one step that cannot be skipped.
2. **Watermark:** free CapCut exports can append a CapCut ending clip; trim it before posting. Pro-asset templates force a watermark unless paid.
3. Fill every slot with our own words and renders; never other accounts' clips.
4. Post as a trial reel; caption from the kit.
