# Hearth on Instagram: reaching the US and Canada from Pakistan

**Version:** 2026-08-22. Supersedes the Instagram parts of `03-platform-strategy.md` where they conflict. Audience: South Asian diaspora in the US and Canada first, widening in month 3 to 4.

## 1. The geography problem, answered with evidence

What Meta has actually said, and what that means for an account run from Pakistan.

1. **Where a Reel goes is decided by engagement, not by your SIM.** Meta's ranking explainer lists viewer activity, your history with the viewer, content signals and poster signals. Country of creation is not a ranking input. Every new post gets a small test audience and expands if it earns watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach (Mosseri, Jan 2025; sends matter most for reaching non-followers).
   Sources: about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained · creators.instagram.com/blog/recommendations-and-originality · socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/
2. **So why do Pakistan-run accounts drift Pakistani?** Because the test audience is seeded from interest and early engagers. If your first followers, likers and commenters are your Pakistani network, the system learns "people like them" and expands from there. Fix the early engagement graph and you fix the geography. (Inference from Meta's statements, consistent with Hootsuite's 2025 experiment: first-hour engagement tripled non-follower reach.)
3. **The "Account based in Pakistan" label is real and not editable.** Instagram shows a primary country on About This Account, and a "Based in" line on posts for accounts whose audience is mostly elsewhere. It is derived from stated location, devices and connection information. Scheduling tools do not change it. A US teammate logging in natively may shift it over months (anecdotal). Meta Verified does not change it.
   Sources: help.instagram.com/697961817256175/ · facebook.com/help/998434327197383/ · help.metricool.com (location label article)
4. **Do not use a VPN or fake a US origin.** Meta's Inauthentic Behavior policy (updated Dec 2025) bans misleading people about the origin of an account. A Pakistani company serving US members is not misrepresentation; pretending to be American is, and VPN-only setups get suppressed reach in operator tests. We state Pakistan openly and make it part of the story: a company built by the people who used to be the ones everyone called.
5. **Hashtags, geotags and posting-time hacks barely matter.** Mosseri: hashtags do not increase reach; posts are capped at 5. No Meta source says geotags route distribution. Posting time gives a modest boost at most.
6. **The only guaranteed geographic lever is paid.** Ads Manager (web, not the iOS app, which adds Apple's 30%) lets you target US and Canada from $1/day for reach, about $5/day for clicks. Health detailed-targeting was removed in 2024 and condition-specific sites lose lower-funnel pixel events, so ads describe the service, never the viewer's condition, and optimise for landing-page views.
7. **Mental-health-adjacent content has a recommendation ceiling.** Content that discusses self-harm, suicide or eating disorders, or trivialises death or depression, is not recommended to non-followers. Support and recovery framing is allowed. Words to keep out of hooks and captions: suicide, self-harm, kill myself, depression (as a label), disorder, trauma (as a hook).
   Source: help.instagram.com/313829416281232
8. **Originality is enforced.** Watermarked or reposted content is ineligible for recommendation; since April 2026 this covers photos and carousels too. Export clean files for every platform.

## 2. The plan at $0

Ranked by evidence. Do these in order; do not skip to number 6.

1. **Professional account, bio that states the offer and the location.** Handle, name, bio and settings are in `instagram-setup.md` (name "Hearth - Someone who stays"; bio opens "For the one everyone calls."). Link: dearhearth.com/?utm_source=ig&utm_medium=profile.
2. **Seed the first 200 followers from the right place, not from your phone contacts.** Do not invite Pakistani friends and family. Do not run a "follow for follow". Instead, before the first post, spend two weeks commenting thoughtfully (no links) on 10 to 15 US/CA diaspora accounts a day: Brown Girl Therapy, South Asian mental-health creators, eldest-daughter and immigrant-parent accounts, Desi mom creators, podcast accounts. `/collab-scout` produces the list. Early engagers shape the test audience.
3. **Trial reels for every new hook.** Trial reels go only to non-followers, so your Pakistani network cannot skew the test. Post every Reel as a trial first; it auto-shares if it performs within 72 hours.
4. **Optimise every Reel for sends.** Forwardable, diaspora-specific, English, under 90 seconds, original footage or original Remotion renders, captions burned in, no watermarks. "Send this to the eldest daughter in your life" is a legitimate closing line when the content earns it.
5. **Collab posts with US and Canadian people.** A Collab appears on both profiles and goes to their followers in feed. Start with members (with consent and a fee), diaspora micro-creators (5k to 50k), and Keepers based in North America. `/collab-scout` finds them; the influencer-marketing skill writes the brief.
6. **Cross-post clean files to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Threads** on the same day. Shorts in particular seeds US search traffic; TikTok is where the diaspora eldest-daughter conversation already lives.
7. **Post on Eastern Time.** Tuesday to Thursday 9 to 11am ET, Sunday 7 to 9pm ET (the Sunday-scaries slot). That is evening and late night in Pakistan; schedule with Metricool or Buffer, which publish via the Graph API with no reach penalty.
8. **When money appears:** the first $150/month goes to web Ads Manager Reels boosts targeted US + Canada, 25 to 45, interests around South Asian culture, immigrant family, caregiving. Run the three best trial reels. Expect restricted pixel events; optimise for landing-page views.
9. **A US-based teammate or Keeper posting natively** from a real US device and SIM is the only non-paid thing that plausibly moves the "Based in" label, and it is anecdotal. Worth doing only if it costs nothing.

## 3. Content system: three lanes, all faceless

Founder and Keepers are not on camera for now. Every Reel is one of three lanes, all producible with Remotion (`02-remotion-capabilities.md`, `remotion-prompts/`) or AI-assisted editing.

| Lane | Share | What it is | Why it travels |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Letter-to** | 40% | Text on cream paper. "A letter to the eldest daughter who…" One tender truth per beat, 12 to 25 seconds, original audio or quiet piano. | Highest save and send rate for this audience. Cheap. Repeatable. |
| **Slow b-roll essay** | 40% | Hands, a mug, a window, a chair pulled out. 45 to 90 second voiceover reading one paragraph. | The literary register that separates Hearth from therapy-influencer content. Watch time. |
| **Viral format** | 20% | One borrowed structure per week: POV text, "things nobody tells you", before/after captions, a trending AI visual style where it fits the tone. | Reach. Must still be original footage or original renders, no watermarks, and must land on one true thing. |

**Founder note to confirm:** you mentioned "dino initiative" style and other viral AI formats. Tell me which references you mean and I will add a fourth lane with a production template.

Carousels: one per week, 8 to 10 slides, the "letter-to" or a plain explainer ("What a Keeper actually does"). Carousels are the save engine; Reels are the reach engine.

Every piece ends on one of three closers, rotated: "Send this to the person who is always the strong one." · "Hearth: one trained listener, yours for years. $39/month. Link in bio." · "If you read this twice, you already know why."

## 5. What we measure (weekly, `/growth-review`)

From Instagram Insights, paste into `docs/marketing/weeks/metrics.csv`: reach, non-follower share of reach, top countries share (US + CA together is the number that matters), sends, saves, average watch time, profile visits, link clicks. From the site: intake starts with `utm_source=ig`.

Decision rules: a format with sends per reach above 1.5% for two weeks gets a second slot; below 0.3% after three posts gets cut; US + CA share below 40% after week 4 means the seeding (step 2) was not done and we redo it before posting more.

## 6. 90 days

- **Weeks 1 to 2:** account set-up, bio, highlights, two weeks of daily comments on the target list, first Remotion templates rendered, no posting yet.
- **Weeks 3 to 8:** 4 Reels + 1 carousel per week, all trial reels. First two Collabs (members or Keepers). Weekly growth review.
- **Weeks 9 to 12:** keep the two formats that travel, drop the third. First creator Collab. If any budget exists, boost the top three Reels to US/CA. Decide whether to widen beyond diaspora based on who is actually sending.

## 7. Guardrails

- Nothing publishes or spends without a human. Agents draft; you approve.
- No crisis content as a hook. Crisis resources link in bio and in any caption touching grief.
- No testimonial without written consent and a fee. No member counts until real.
- No clinical claims, no "treat", no "heal". Peer support, said plainly.
- Copy rules in `AGENTS.md`: no em dashes, plain first.
