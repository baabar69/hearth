# Instagram account setup · Hearth

One-time checklist. Do it in this order. Nothing here needs a VPN, a US number, or anything Meta's policies dislike. Assets are in `brand/instagram/`.

## 1. Before you create anything

- Use a fresh email on the domain (hello@dearhearth.com or instagram@dearhearth.com via ImprovMX), not your personal Gmail. You will want to hand this account to a teammate one day.
- Use your real phone number. The account will show "Based in Pakistan" regardless; pretending otherwise violates Meta's inauthentic-behaviour policy and gets reach suppressed. We own it in the bio instead.
- Do this on a clean phone profile or a phone that has never logged into your personal Instagram, so the app does not auto-suggest your Pakistani friends and contacts.

## 3. Right after creation: the skip list

These are the steps that make a Pakistan-run account look Pakistani to the algorithm. Skip every one.

- **Skip "Connect to Facebook" and "Find friends".** Do not sync contacts. Do not follow anyone from your phone's contacts.
- **Do not follow friends and family** and do not ask them to follow you yet. The first 200 followers should come from US and Canada diaspora accounts (see `weeks/2026-W35/collab-targets.md`). Family can follow in month three.
- **Do not follow 200 accounts in a day.** Follow the 26 target accounts over a week, ten minutes at a time, while commenting.
- **Do not post for two weeks.** Comment daily on the target list first so your early engagers are the right people.

## 4. Settings to change (Settings and activity)

- **Account privacy:** public.
- **Two-factor authentication:** on, authenticator app, and save the backup codes in the password manager. Add a recovery email.
- **Threads:** the account is created alongside; claim the same handle there too and leave it for cross-posting.
- **Reels and remixes:** allow remixing on (original audio compounds when others use it). Allow trial reels (Professional dashboard, Reels).
- **Original audio:** when you upload a Reel, name the audio "Hearth · <piece name>" so remixes point back.
- **Suggested accounts:** leave "Show account suggestions on profiles" on; it is how similar US accounts surface yours.
- **Sensitive content control:** standard. Nothing to change.
- **Branded content tools:** turn on once a Collab involves money (FTC disclosure).

## 5. Meta Business Suite (business.facebook.com)

- Create a Business Portfolio named Hearth, add the Instagram account, add yourself as admin and a backup admin.
- Set the portfolio time zone to **America/New_York** so scheduling and Insights line up with the plan (all slots in the playbook are ET).
- Connect a scheduler: Business Suite's own planner is free; Metricool (free tier) or Buffer if you want one queue for Instagram, TikTok, Shorts and Threads. Schedulers publish through the official API and do not affect reach or the location label.
- Do not create an ad account yet. When budget appears, create it inside this portfolio and run boosts from the web, not the iOS app (Apple adds 30%).

## 6. Profile furniture (before the first post)

- **Highlights** (covers in `brand/instagram/`): Start here · Keepers · Friday notes · Stories · How it works. Create each from a first Story: "Start here" gets a three-frame explainer (what a Keeper is, what you get, the price); "How it works" gets the friend / Keeper / therapist distinction and the crisis line.
- **Pinned posts** (after week one): the carousel, the letter-to Reel, and a plain "What Hearth is" post.
- **Link in bio:** the site, not a Linktree. Update the `utm_campaign` each week if you want per-week attribution.

## 7. Week one and two: engagement only

- 30 minutes a day in US morning hours (6 to 8pm Pakistan time): ten specific comments on the target list, no links, no pitching. Reply to replies.
- Follow the 26 accounts gradually. Save posts from them (saves teach the algorithm what you are near).
- Watch Stories of the same accounts; answer a few question stickers with one real line.
- Keep a note of which accounts reply to you; they are your first Collab conversations.

## 8. First post (start of week three)

- Sunday 7pm ET, the letter-to Reel from `weeks/2026-W35/`, posted as a **trial reel** first.
- Before posting, run the checklist in `07-instagram-playbook.md` section 7 and the batch self-check.
- Then `/growth-review` the following Monday with the numbers from Insights (Reach by country is under Insights, Audience, once you have 100 followers; per-post Reach appears on each Reel).

## What the account will look like to a US visitor

Handle `@thedearhearth`, name "Hearth - Someone who stays", a cairn on cream paper, a bio that says what it costs and that it is built in Pakistan for the diaspora, five highlights, and a feed of quiet text-on-paper Reels about the eldest daughter. That is the intended impression: a small, serious, literate service that is not pretending to be anything else.
