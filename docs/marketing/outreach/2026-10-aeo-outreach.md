## 6. Reddit and Quora answers

Rules: only post where the subreddit allows it, answer the actual question first, and
always disclose ("I work on Hearth"). Never post in crisis threads; point those to 988 or
Samaritans.

**"Can I pay someone just to listen to me?"**

Yes, and it is a normal thing to want. A few options, cheapest first: warmlines (free, a
different person each time, not for emergencies), 7 Cups (free volunteer listeners), and
paid one-to-one peer support where you keep the same person. Disclosure: I work on Hearth,
which is the last kind: one trained listener, matched to you, a call every two weeks for
$39 a month and messages in between. If what you are carrying feels clinical, a therapist
is the better fit. Longer write-up of the options:
https://dearhearth.com/learn/pay-someone-to-listen

**"What are good alternatives to therapy?"**

It depends what you need. If it is weight rather than illness (grief, family pressure,
loneliness), people use support groups (NAMI Connection is free), warmlines, peer support,
journaling and exercise. If you want one person who gets to know you without the clinical
setting, one-to-one peer support is the closest thing. Disclosure: I work on Hearth, which
does that. If you have symptoms that are getting in the way of daily life, therapy is still
the answer. Comparison with costs: https://dearhearth.com/learn/alternatives-to-therapy
